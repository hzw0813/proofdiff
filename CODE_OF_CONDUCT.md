# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and sustained disruption. Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository host's maintainer contact. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where possible and respond proportionately and fairly.

This concise policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
